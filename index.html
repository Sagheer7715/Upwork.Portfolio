Critical compliance areas
This is the most overlooked risk for new beauty sellers. Non-compliance leads to listing removal, account suspension, or legal liability. Budget time and money here.

FDA classification: cosmetic vs. drug

Urea nail gel → cosmetic IF claims are cosmetic-only
Allowed: "softens nails", "moisturizes", "improves appearance"
Becomes an OTC drug if you claim to treat or cure
AVOID: "treats nail fungus", "cures onychomycosis", "kills bacteria" — these trigger drug classification
"Nail fungus treatment" keyword on listings is gray area
Many competitors use it and get away with it — but Amazon increasingly suppresses such listings. Not recommended for new sellers.

Amazon beauty category requirements

Products must be brand new (no refurbished)
Full ingredient list in INCI format required
Manufacturer address on packaging
Expiry date in MM-YYYY or MM-DD-YYYY format
Safety seals on topical products strongly recommended
Topicals may require category approval (gated)

Required documentation

Certificate of Analysis (COA) from manufacturer
Safety Data Sheet (SDS) if applicable
Manufacturer invoices (3 x 10-unit minimum for approval)
GMP-compliant manufacturer preferred
California Safe Cosmetics Act compliance if selling in CA
FDA MoCRA registration (mandatory since Dec 2023)
MoCRA — Modernization of Cosmetics Regulation Act (2023)
Facility registration with FDA now mandatory for cosmetics manufacturers
This applies to your contract manufacturer — confirm they are registered
Product listing with FDA required
Submit via FDA Cosmetics Direct portal — free to file
Serious Adverse Event reporting
Must report within 15 business days if a consumer reports a serious adverse event
Safe claim examples: "Softens thick toenails" · "Moisturizes and conditions nail plate" · "Restores healthy nail appearance" · "Supports nail health" · "Gentle exfoliant for rough, thickened nails"

Get COA + safety testing done. Place first production run (300–500 units). Finalize listing copy, A+ Content, 7–9 listing images. Register for Amazon Brand Registry.
Month 4–5
Launch & initial reviews
Launch with PPC (exact + broad campaigns). Use "Vine" program (15 units) for early reviews. Set competitive price ($1–3 below top competitors). Run a 10–15% coupon.
Month 6–8
Optimize & expand
Review ACOS weekly. A/B test main image. Add Subscribe & Save. Launch keratin serum (Product 2). Begin building external traffic via TikTok/Instagram content.
Month 9–12
Scale & brand building
Launch bundle kit. Consider men's line. Build email list via product inserts. Test influencer partnerships with nail health/dermatology micro-influencers. Explore Walmart marketplace.
